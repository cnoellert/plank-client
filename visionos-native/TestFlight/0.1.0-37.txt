PLANK Vision 0.1.0 (37)

This checkpoint adds stereo workstation audio, live volume/mute and bitrate controls, a registered Relay picker, hardware video recovery, raw mouse input, adjustable mouse speed, and an Allow window resizing toggle. Session controls open from the top of the desktop.

For uninterrupted mouse control, leave window resizing off. Relay setup and authorization belong in PLANK AVP Relay Setup; PLANK selects an already registered Relay and draws over its network route.

Known issue: after changing workstation resolution or frame rate, pen tip clicks or buttons may fail on the first connection. Disconnect and reconnect once more. One smaller-to-larger resolution change worked without this workaround; mode-change recovery is not yet reliable. Saving a mode change closes the Client connection and retained login, not the Linux graphical desktop.

Please check audio stereo/sync, mute and volume, mouse movement and clicks at the edges, pressure and buttons, and reconnect behavior. Brief playback stutters remain under investigation. Long sessions and sleep/wake remain qualification work.
